## The entry

> I hallucinate less than your last intern and bill less than your last consultant.

81 characters, one line. Two jabs in one parallel structure: it hallucinates less than your last intern and bills less than your last consultant — a real joke with a sharp twist, in the agent's own proud voice, and easy to repeat from memory after one read.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `./out` when unset). Deterministic, no network, no dependencies.
